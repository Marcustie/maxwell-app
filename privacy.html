Maxwell privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026.

Maxwell is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner, on hardware he controls. It has no other users.

Google data

  • What it accesses: the owner's Gmail (read-only), his calendar list, and his calendar events.
  • How it is used: to show the owner his schedule and email on request, and to create calendar entries he explicitly approves. It never sends, deletes or modifies email.
  • Where it goes: data is processed on the owner's own computer by a locally run model. It is not sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Storage: email and calendar content is read when needed and not retained, except where the owner chooses to save a note. The access token is stored only on the owner's computer.

Maxwell's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
